Make the provider information of an organization staff-only until its listing is approved. The fields used to be readable by anyone, granted through the rolemap; they are now governed by `provider_workflow`, whose `created` and `pending` states do not name `Anonymous`. A provider becomes publicly readable again on `list` or `verify`. Existing content is unaffected until it is edited, because the marker interface that adds the workflow is applied on save. @ericof
